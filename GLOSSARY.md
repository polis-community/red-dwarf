# Red-Dwarf

Terms distinguishing Red-Dwarf's analysis pipelines from the platforms associated
with them.

## Language

**Polis-compatible pipeline**:
Red-Dwarf's analysis variant targeting legacy Polis behavior within its documented
compatibility limits.
_Avoid_: Polis, when referring to this pipeline rather than the platform.

**Agora enhanced pipeline**:
Red-Dwarf's analysis variant with targeted improvements to statement selection and
consensus scoring, rather than a goal of reproducing legacy Polis behavior.
_Avoid_: Agora, when referring to this pipeline rather than the platform.

**Polis platform**:
The upstream Polis participatory sensemaking platform, distinct from Red-Dwarf's
Polis-compatible pipeline.

**Agora platform**:
The Agora Citizen Network platform, distinct from Red-Dwarf's Agora enhanced
pipeline.
